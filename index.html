DETERMINISTIC SAFETY

Aviation-Safety Architecture

The LLM explains in natural language, but never decides whether to alert. Deterministic threshold logic controls all physical safety.

1. Deterministic Layer (Authority):

Distance thresholding · Multi-frame persistence · Walking corridor filter · Validated on 40 recorded hazard clips.

2. On-Device LLM Layer (Context Only):

Generates natural spoken guidance. Never overrides deterministic safety verdicts.
